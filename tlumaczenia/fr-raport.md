# Rapport — fr (France)

- Tense unified: the whole story is told in passé composé/imparfait. Sections 15–18 had been in the present tense and were rewritten. The hook (1–3) stays in the present, as a flash-forward, and so does the moral in section 19.
- Viewer address: "vous" everywhere (96, 110, 332, 334). 332/334 had used "tu".
- Tournament awards are now "le trophée du meilleur joueur / meilleur buteur du tournoi" (106, 177, 178, 263), so they can't be mistaken for the Ballon d'Or (147, 149, 160, 218, 274).
- "pecho frío" is glossed "Un cœur froid" (168), the same as in 329. "Poitrine froide" is not idiomatic French.
- Terms: "message" for the farewell note everywhere (5, 8, 122, 215, 310, 325); "serviette en papier" (60) → "serviette" (61, 308); senior national team = "l'équipe A" (107, 125, 233); club = "entraîneur", Argentina = "sélectionneur" ("coach" removed in 74 and 217).
- "Phase finale" is ambiguous for the World Cup, so knockouts are now "élimination directe" (97, 287). "Tirs au but" is used for shoot-outs and "penalty" for a penalty in open play.
- Restored: "Mario" Götze (176), "pour l'Argentine" (187), and in 327 "traversé l'océan avec lui". 327 also fixes "n'y ont jamais cru", which could be read as "never believed in him". 326 "inconnu" → "étranger chez lui".
- Child-friendly: 73 "Mon vieux" → "Mon papa"; 30/33 now indirect speech.
- Kept as they were (worth checking): 157 "je t'embrasse" for "un abrazo" (a warm, normal French sign-off); 76 "el enano, le nain"; 250 "idiot"; 338 "le pleurnicheur"; 316 "guerre des Malouines" (the standard French name).
- Hard to fit: 263, 278, 300, 322, 327. In 327 the grandmother's line is shortened to "qui l'a fait jouer"; in 275 "often" was dropped.
